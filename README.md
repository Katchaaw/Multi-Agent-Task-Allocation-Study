# Multi-Robot Task Allocation

Research project studying **dynamic Multi-Robot Task Allocation (MRTA)**, with a focus on **constraints, uncertainty and decentralized coordination**.

## 📌 Overview

Multi-Robot Task Allocation (MRTA) is a central problem in multi-robot systems. It consists in assigning tasks to a fleet of autonomous robots while optimizing global objectives such as execution time, cost, energy consumption, coverage or robustness.

Real-world applications include:

* 🤖 Autonomous logistics
* 🗺️ Exploration of unknown environments
* 🚨 Search and rescue
* 👁️ Surveillance
* 🤝 Human-robot assistance

The problem becomes particularly challenging when tasks and environments are **dynamic, uncertain or subject to additional constraints**.

This project provides a **critical review of foundational and recent approaches** to dynamic MRTA, organized around the different types of constraints and uncertainties encountered in multi-robot systems.

## 🎯 Objectives

The study aims to:

* Analyze the foundations and evolution of the **MRTA problem**.
* Review major approaches to **distributed task allocation**.
* Study how constraints and uncertainty affect task allocation.
* Analyze approaches addressing **temporal, spatial, motion and collision constraints**.
* Investigate allocation in dynamic and uncertain environments.
* Study recent approaches involving **human-aware task allocation**.
* Identify common principles, differences and limitations across existing methods.
* Highlight current challenges and open research questions.

## 🔬 Focus: SCOBA

A reference approach, **SCOBA**, is studied in detail as a representative method for task allocation under temporal constraints and uncertainty.

The analysis covers:

* The problem formulation and assumptions.
* The underlying allocation strategy.
* The algorithm and its main mechanisms.
* Its handling of constraints and uncertainty.
* Performance guarantees and limitations.
* Comparison with other approaches from the literature.

## 🧩 State of the Art

The reviewed approaches cover several families of MRTA methods, including:

### Distributed Allocation

* Auction-based approaches
* Consensus-based approaches
* Distributed optimization
* Decentralized coordination

### Constraints

* ⏱️ Temporal constraints
* 🗺️ Spatial constraints
* 💥 Collision and motion constraints
* 🤖 Robot capabilities and heterogeneity
* 👥 Human-related constraints

### Uncertainty

* Environmental uncertainty
* Temporal uncertainty
* Dynamic task arrival
* Partial knowledge
* Uncertain behavior of other agents

The analysis highlights the evolution of MRTA from relatively structured allocation problems toward **large-scale, dynamic and uncertain multi-robot environments**.
